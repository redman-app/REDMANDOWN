What’s inside the little guy

Screen control, system tools, custom animations and a pile of safety rules.

eyes.app
👁️

Sees your screen

Looks at a screenshot each turn, then takes exactly one step: click, type, press a key, open a URL, launch an allowed app or create a file.

system_tools.dll
🗂️

System-wide tools

List folders, read text and code files, check system info, read the clipboard – anywhere, read-only.

moves.json
🎬

AI-made animations BETA

Your AI writes a tiny choreography plan. Redman’s engine performs it. Plans are data, never code.

shields.sys
🛡️

Risk-rated actions

LOW runs by itself, MEDIUM asks, HIGH always asks, BLOCKED never runs. Esc stops everything instantly.

brains.cfg
🔌

Bring your own AI

Gemini and Groq built in, plus Custom AI APIs BETA. A backup provider is always announced, never silent.

vault.key

devtools.css
